SEO FOUNDATIONS
Every page has a descriptive title, description, language, viewport and one h1.
Storefront content is present in HTML before JavaScript. Internal links are
crawlable. Indexable content pages have canonical URLs, social preview metadata,
lightweight WebSite/WebPage JSON-LD and sitemap.xml entries. Images include
useful alt text, dimensions and lazy loading except the primary hero image.
No invented ratings, certifications or commercial offers are marked up.
Register, checkout and admin have noindex metadata. robots.txt allows crawlers
to see those directives. A private hosted deployment would require access and could not
be publicly indexed. Public search visibility requires a public deployment.
When moving to a real domain, replace the current hostname in canonical and
Open Graph tags, JSON-LD, robots.txt and sitemap.xml. Confirm real business data
and submit the final sitemap through Google Search Console when public.
SEO does not guarantee search rankings.
Reference: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide
