The business behind the coffee

A direct-to-customer concept. Gera Coffee is primarily a B2C store because it offers products to individual consumers. Cafés and business buyers represent a planned B2B audience. Our revenue model is direct product sales, with bulk coffee sales as a potential secondary channel.

01

Customer segments

Specialty coffee enthusiasts, premium home consumers, and cafés or business buyers.

02

Value propositions

Ethiopian Arabica in whole-bean and ground formats, considered presentation, and clear product choices.

03

Channels

A direct website storefront, social media, and personal contact for business enquiries. All channels are conceptual.

04

Customer relationships

Helpful product information, direct contact, and a planned post-purchase support experience.

05

Revenue streams

Direct coffee product sales to individuals; a planned secondary route for café and bulk orders.

06

Key resources

The brand, coffee inventory, product photography, the storefront, and future supplier relationships.

07

Key activities

Product selection, listings, a packing and fulfilment concept, marketing, and customer communication.

08

Key partners

Prospective coffee suppliers, delivery services, and payment providers. No partnerships or integrations are currently claimed.

09

Cost structure

Coffee purchasing, packaging, delivery, marketing, and website operations.
